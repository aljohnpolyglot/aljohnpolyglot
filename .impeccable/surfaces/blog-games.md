# Polyglot Papers — Arcade Field Manual

## Direction contract

- **Mode:** Read.
- **Thesis:** The article behaves like an arcade operator's field manual: every game screen is evidence, every rule is a control label, and the reader advances through three playable chapters.
- **First viewport:** The shared white navbar sits above a near-black split hero. A monumental condensed headline occupies the left manual panel; an original text-free arcade-language machine fills the right visual panel. The three selection rules form the lower control rail.
- **Visitor path:** Claim → three selection rules → personal explanation of what made the games different → clear counterexamples → BitLife for everyday choices → Cell to Singularity for technical context → Plague Inc. for precision under pressure → transferable closing advice.
- **Palette:** ink `#071018`, cabinet `#0b1721`, warm paper `#f1eadb`, signal yellow `#f4c430`, red `#e33d2e`, cyan `#3fb7d8`.
- **Type:** self-hosted Barlow Condensed Bold for display and controls; Barlow for reading copy.
- **Materials:** powder-coated cabinet metal, printed operator labels, one-pixel rules, registration marks, restrained screen glow. Square geometry; no decorative glass, gradients, rounded app cards, or fake metrics.
- **Media:** an original generated hero image plus verified official Google Play and PlayStation media. All three game galleries use German-localized store screenshots. The five supplied social JPGs remain private visual references and never ship.
- **Signature interaction:** each chapter consolidates five complete screenshots into one keyboard-focusable, scroll-snap carousel with explicit previous/next controls, while a thin reading-progress filament tracks the article.
- **Responsive rule:** the split hero stacks into one column, galleries preserve full screenshots with `object-fit: contain`, and the document never overflows horizontally.
- **Approved comp:** `.impeccable/mocks/polyglot-papers-games-01-arcade-field-manual.png`.
- **Risk:** arcade chrome can overpower a long essay; reading measure and quiet chapter copy must remain primary.
