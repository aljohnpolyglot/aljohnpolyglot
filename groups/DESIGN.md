# Language community surfaces

## Shared contract

Community pages may share structure and accessibility behavior, but not a single visual skin.

- Always keep the shared global navbar and footer. A community index may sit beneath the global navbar as a second navigation layer.
- Use authentic logos and real documentary media for the people, teacher, group, and meetings represented.
- Give meaningful photo sets a dedicated carousel or slideshow with visible controls, status, keyboard behavior, descriptive alt text, and a visible `×` in the enlarged-image view.
- Modals and lightboxes close by visible control and Escape, contain focus, restore focus, and never overflow the viewport horizontally.
- Use `min-width: 0`, responsive columns, and contained media so the document itself has no horizontal overflow.
- The main `groups.html` directory is logo-only. Member, meetup, and teacher photographs belong inside dedicated group pages.

## Tropamici

Tropamici is a warm Italian editorial memory of conversation moving from a group chat to a shared table.

- Type: `Fraunces` for editorial display and `DM Sans` for navigation, body, and controls.
- Materials: cream paper, dark espresso fields, Italian green, restrained coral, and antique gold details.
- Place the Tropamici logo in the copy column; it must never overlap the hero photograph or its caption.
- Lead with the real meetup photograph and a clear, low-pressure description of how conversation becomes community.
- Keep the two meetup photographs in their own navigable gallery with thumbnails and an enlarged view.
- The join block may use the Messenger invitation and QR code, but should emphasize participation rather than passive group membership.

## Filipinhos

Filipinhos is a Brazilian-modernist Portuguese-for-Filipinos surface. It must remain visibly distinct from Tropamici and the Italian page.

- Type: `Bricolage Grotesque` for expressive display and `Manrope` for reading and utility text.
- Palette: saturated Brazilian green, solar yellow, deep blue, warm off-white, and sparing orange-red accents.
- Use bold planes, offset frames, and modernist asymmetry rather than parchment, Renaissance line work, or Italian editorial ornament.
- Keep the hero copy spacious. Do not restore the removed `BR · PH` badge.
- Compose the Filipinhos logo and Professor Bruno portrait as supporting visual anchors without compressing the narrative column.
- Professor Bruno receives a dedicated section explaining the Brazil/Philippines, Tagalog, and Baybayin bridge.
- Embed the supplied Facebook reel as the central watch experience and retain a direct Facebook link for cross-origin or sign-in fallbacks.
- Use the supplied Messenger invitation in the join actions and QR section.

## Identity boundary

Reuse navigation, accessibility, data hygiene, and tested interaction mechanics. Do not copy typography, palette, ornament, card geometry, image treatment, or motion character between communities merely because they share a page template.
