Meet the community

Portuguese practice, with a Filipino point of reference

Filipinhos bridges Brazil and the Philippines through scheduled video calls, group discussions, speaking practice, and conversations about Brazilian history and culture.

Professor Bruno Gomes lived in the Philippines and speaks Tagalog. His work with Baybayin gives the group a guide who can connect Portuguese to languages and cultural references Filipino learners already know.

From passive input to active use

How the community keeps Portuguese moving

The structure is deliberately light enough to join from anywhere, but social enough that Portuguese does not remain trapped in an app.

  1. 01

    Active group chats

    Memes, questions, and study help keep contact frequent between sessions.

  2. 02

    Virtual meetups

    Calls make the group accessible while giving speaking a real audience.

  3. 03

    Brazilian culture

    History and culture keep vocabulary attached to a place and a point of view.

  4. 04

    Filipino reference points

    Explanations can begin from what learners already know instead of erasing it.
